Sequence Change & Molecular Mechanism Explanation:

Sequence Change Mechanism: This is a missense mutation caused by a single nucleotide substitution (G to T) at coding position 447 (c.447G>T) in the NM_000258.3 transcript. This changes codon 149 from ATG (Methionine) to ATT (Isoleucine).

Protein Level Effect: At the protein level (NP_000249.1), residue 149 changes from methionine to isoleucine (p.Met149Ile). Because MYL3 encodes the ventricular essential light chain of cardiac myosin, altering this residue can disrupt the stability of the myosin neck region, affecting muscle contraction and force generation in the heart.
